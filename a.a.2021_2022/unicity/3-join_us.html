new investor

We understand that a one-of-a-kind environment like Unicity is rare to find on the outside. If you're an outside business owner who has been captured by the allure of Unicity, there's good news for you. To support this ecosystem, we've decided to open the doors of the city to a few lucky outside picks.

You can invest in a business in one of our four districts or you can donate an amount at your discretion to become part of the city's official sponsors. In both cases, the procedure is the same:

1) Send us a detailed email specifying what is your interest among those previously listed;
2) Your email will be analyzed by the Investors Committee of Unicity, which will respond in a timely manner;
3) In case of positive feedback from the Investors Committee, you will be called for an interview;
4) The outcome of the interview will be communicated via email within a week.


A procedure of this magnitude allows us to guarantee profitable and optimal collaborations with outsiders for both parties.

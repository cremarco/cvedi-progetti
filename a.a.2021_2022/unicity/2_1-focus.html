to focus

At Unicity, everyone can be a better version of himself. A universe of chances, twenty-two square kilometers of pure focus.

libraries and study rooms

Best places to study in. Thirty-two libraries and more than forty study rooms all over Unicity designed to improve productivity of students. Absolute silence is required in the libraries.

anechoic room

An anechoic chamber is a room designed to absorb reflections of sound or electromagnetic waves. This provides isolation from the outside and it allows students to carry on their tasks without being influenced by external or internal reflected noise.

rooms for creative development

These rooms were inspired by Google Space Works and they give unicitizens a creative space where ideas can flow freely to form unexpected connections that transform into tools and projects. In here, students can be both comfortable and dynamic.

time room

Time stops in here.
The most exclusive place in whole Unicity. Click here for more information

labs

Fifteen innovative labs that have all the means to let students make experiments, researches and pratices of all kinds. Every week, there are also courses to let students from non-scientific faculties learn how to work and orientate in this kind of places.

virtual room

Technology and AI meet student’s needs in here. Virtual room offers the best available technologies for students (AI students mainly even it’s open to everyone) to improve their productivity and creativity wrapping them in the charm of the future.

concentration pods

Acoustic comfort has a fundamental role in the liveability of the environments where our students study. Inspired by Google Space Works ones, pods guarantee wellness in study places and allow self-reflection and isolation.

hammock room

“Depending on the kind of work you do, a hammock chair or cushions on the floor might be a better fit than an office chair,” Paola Marella says. We couldn’t agree more, and for that reason we wanted to offer our students this Hammock Room.

city hall

The headquarter of Unicity, the City Hall, is located excatly at the center of the town. Here is possible to receive any kind of help or information a student needs. Our principal Leonardo Bentivoglio lives in here.

supermarkets

Besides groceries located all around Unicity, in this District there are three futuristic Supermarkets to meet the needs of the students. It is possible to use the service Book and Take-away or the Delivery Market one.

shops

World's most famous brands, including Unicity, are scattered throughout the Shops Neighborhood, where 20+ maxi stores are located. Floors over floors, shops over shops, this neighborhood offers the best opportunities for shopping.

security service

Even if Unicity’s crime stats are the lowest in Europe, it can not exist a city with a similar ecosystem without a proper Security Service. It is possible to reach them just by calling the number 555 on your phone.

healthcare

With 300+ professional doctors and 500+ internship students, every unicitizen is in safe hands. Every student can reach Healthcare services by calling the same number of Security Services (555).

means of transport

USA’s technologies, Japan’s punctuality, Italian design. Unicity got the best means of transport of the world, and all of them are eco-friendly. Trams, metros, trains, busses and other twelve kind of means of transport are used everyday by more than 50.000 Unicitizens.

banks

Thirty-five banks, one hundred twenty-five cash machines and more than one hundred bank workers in order to help Unicitizens and all their economic needs. In each of them the changing currency service is avaiable.

residences

There are mainly three ways to live in Unicity: hotels, apartments or dormitory rooms. They are all located in the Residences Neighborhood, the largest neighborhood in Unicity (98k people living in here).

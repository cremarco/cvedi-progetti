our currency

Krèmata is Unicity’s official currency and its value is proportional to Euro. Its existence enforces our city’s independence, autonomy and economy. The currency is valid only between our walls; therefore, its validity is inexistent outside Unicity. It would be only a souvenir.

The currency exchance is possible in one of our thirty-five banks in Unicity, online or by phone. If it’s your first time, we suggest you to come in the Basics District, where you’ll immediately receive your Kremata credit card. You can also use the online platform or the “Kremata-Unicity” home banking app.

Using any other value is not acceptable and, vice versa, Krèmata is not valid outside Unicity. The currency exchange is only possible via app, in one of our banks located in the Basics Discrict or in one of the 125 cash machines. Every usage of Krèmata will be automatically recorded in our systems.

find a way

to have fun

Having fun here is not an option, it’s a modus vivendi. An immerging and limitless world where everything has a new, exciting taste.

football

Football is one of the main sport disciplines in Unicity It’s a sport that requires perseverance, self-denial, hard work, sacrifice, dedication and respect for authority. Each faculty has its own team, which can partecipate to different tournaments, both inside and outside our walls.

basketball

A sport where tossing, decision making and technique matter the most. Our six pavillons allow students to play unselfish, play together and play hard in both formal and informal occasions.

ritual spaces

Unicity allows students to find the perfect place to just go and relax. In our spaces, students will both disconnect, reconnect and look around themselves. Overcome their internal chaos and calm down on a daily basis is possible, just choose how and where.

golf

The area covers over 33,000 square meters in which there are two distinct structures: one dedicated to the long game and one to the short one. This is a sport that teaches humility, emphaty, but most importantly patience. In golf, the most important shot is the next one.

volleyball

Volleyball is the third most played sport in Unicity. Like football, each faculty has built its own team and has its own professional coaches. Getting served takes on a different, deeper and sporty meaning..

tennis

With more than thirty tennis fields (in grass, clay and artifical grass) tennis became on of the most popular individual sports at Unicity. In every sport a clear mind is what matters to most, especially in tennis.

show jumping

Without any doubt show jumping is one of the most underrated sports in the world, but not in here. With our three stables and 100+ partecipants per year, show jumping is revealed to be fun, competitive and entertaining as much as any other popular sport.

hockey

Ice hockey is the latest addition to Unicity, but not the least important. This sport is facing more and more attention nowadays, as the increasing numbers of athletes confirms. Four fields and more to come, we’re just getting started.

sex neighborhood

In Unicity taboos just don’t exist. Conscious about the relevance of sex, the idea here is to create the safest place where everyone can have fun in that way, without taking any kind of risks. To be completely aware of what is going on in there, the best advice we can give is to take the time to visit it.

clubs

The core business of the Fun District is with no doubt the Clubs’ neighborhood. More than thirty clubs with every type of music, any kind of people, different cultures and mindsets to meet everyone’s needs and desires.

arena

We’re not based in Rome but, while in the Arena, it could feel like being at the Colosseo. This is the place where Unicity’s biggest events take place, where thousands of students forget their problems, enjoy the moment and create indelible memories.

rooftop bars

Six rooftop bars, each one with a different concept, from the “Space” one, to the “2150” one. Most of the graduation parties starts there and some even say that Rooftop Bars neighborhood is the coolest one in Unicity.

amusement park

The third biggest amusement park of Europe is located in Unicity’s Fun District. With approximately 3000+ visitors per day, this is one of the busiest places of this city. This is perfectly understandable, as there are 70+ different attractions and the ticket costs only 15 Krèmata.

free drugs building

Prohibition has never led to great results as history teaches. Unicity’s Free Drugs Building, supervised by experts, doctors and psychologists, is the perfect place to give free rein to our citizens whenever they need it. Entrances are limited.

artificial beach

When summer comes, chilling at the Artificial Beach can give some Venice Beach vibes, as many students say. Sorrounded by beach volley, beach football fields and nine bars, this Artificial Beach is the perfect place either to chill or to have fun.

sky cinema

Some 80’s vibes meeting futuristic cinema solutions. New films every week, marathons of the most popular series, tons of pop corns, all of that under the stars. Tickets available for 5 Krèmata.
